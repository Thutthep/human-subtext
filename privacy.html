Privacy policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This policy covers the website you're reading and the two upload tools behind the Human Subtext YouTube channel and Facebook Page, both called "Human Subtext Uploader": one uses the YouTube Data API, the other is a Meta (Facebook) app.

Who uses the tools

Only the person who runs Human Subtext. The tools upload our own videos, set their titles, descriptions and publish times, and read basic numbers about those videos (views, plays, watch time) so we can see how they're doing. Nobody else signs in to them, and they aren't offered to the public.

What data we handle

  • Our own account access. Sign-in tokens for our own YouTube channel and our own Facebook Page. They're stored only on our own computer, never on this website or in any shared place.
  • Our own videos and their statistics. Video files we made, their text, and aggregate numbers YouTube and Facebook report for them.
  • Nothing about viewers. The tools don't collect, store, sell or share personal information about people who watch, comment or visit. This website has no cookies, analytics, forms or trackers.

Google and YouTube

The YouTube tool uses YouTube API Services. By watching our videos you're also covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service. Our use of data received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Access can be removed at any time from Google security settings.

Meta and Facebook

The Meta app posts Reels to the Human Subtext Page and reads that Page's own video insights. It doesn't use Facebook Login for anyone else and doesn't read other people's profiles. Facebook's own data use is described in the Meta Privacy Policy.

Data deletion

Because the tools only hold access to our own accounts, there's no viewer data to delete. If you ever connected anything to "Human Subtext Uploader" and want it removed:

  1. Facebook: go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites (or Business integrations), find "Human Subtext Uploader" and click Remove.
  2. Google: open myaccount.google.com/permissions, choose "Human Subtext Uploader" and remove access.
  3. Or send a message to the Human Subtext Facebook Page asking us to delete anything we hold about you. We'll reply within 30 days and confirm once it's done.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. Questions: message the Human Subtext Facebook Page.

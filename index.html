HUMAN SUBTEXT

Psychology, told as stories.

Human Subtext makes short illustrated videos about everyday psychology: why we say yes too fast, why nobody stops to help, why one idea gets applause and the same idea doesn't. Every story is made up; the study behind it is real and named on screen.

About the upload tools

We use two small private tools to put our own finished videos on our own YouTube channel and Facebook Page: "Human Subtext Uploader" for the YouTube Data API and a Meta app of the same name for Facebook Reels. They are used only by the person who runs this channel. They don't sign in visitors and don't collect anything from viewers. Details are in the privacy policy.
